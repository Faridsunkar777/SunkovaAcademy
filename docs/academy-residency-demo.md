# Sunkova Academy — Residency Demo (Condensed Page)

Internal demo working concept. Admissions, fees, timing and module assignments remain subject to founding-team decisions.

## What this update changes

The `/residency` page now prioritises scanning. It is intentionally shorter **without changing the existing Academy visual system, homepage, navigation or footer**.

1. **Hero:** One-sentence offer and a three-line snapshot: who, how and proof.
2. **Why it matters:** Two quick reader lenses for learners and partners/supporters.
3. **Design thinking:** Four visible FigJam stages, with the original 10-part board checklist available under **View all 10 FigJam board requirements**. A mentor must approve discovery before implementation.
4. **Six-month journey:** Six compact milestones with one short focus and one output each.
5. **Outcomes:** Five short, reviewable deliverables.
6. **Enquiry:** One concise status statement and collapsed practical notes. Enquiry opens an email draft; no online application backend is provided.

## Two-minute co-founder click path

- Open `/residency`. Show the promise and **at a glance** panel (20 seconds).
- Scroll to **Beyond the portfolio**. Highlight separate learner and partner outcomes (20 seconds).
- Scroll to **Why should this product exist?**. Explain the FigJam/mentor gate; expand the 10-point checklist only if asked (30 seconds).
- Scroll to **From problem to product**. Point out the 26-week sequence and each stage's output (30 seconds).
- Show the graduate evidence and the proposed-cohort enquiry. Expand **Practical program notes** only for detailed questions (20 seconds).

## Invariants

- Only the residency route, residency content data, this guide and a source-level test are changed.
- No new dependencies, CSS, images or animations; no edits to Academy homepage, navigation, footer or course catalogue.
- No promise of admissions, employment, confirmed dates or access to unsupervised client systems.
