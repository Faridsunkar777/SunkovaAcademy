Our promise: You'll leave with things you built, people who've seen them, and the confidence to build the next one.

We follow a challenge-based model. Lectures alone won't get you hired. Every module opens with a Big Idea, becomes personal through an Essential Question, and commits you to a Challenge that ends in a portfolio artefact.

The academy sits beside Sunkova — a design and technology studio in Nairobi working globally. That means real briefs, demo guests, and a path into professional practice.

Whether you join a live cohort or move self-paced, the standard is the same: investigate, build, show, and ship.

  • Beginner-honest

    Clear prerequisites, bridge modules when needed, and no gatekeeping jargon without a build.

  • Portfolio by default

    Case studies, repos, Figma files, red-team reports, and demo films — not slide decks alone.

  • Africa-rooted, globally fluent

    M-Pesa, multilingual UX, low-end devices, and Kenya data-protection context — plus tools used worldwide.

  • Studio sibling

    Learn next to practitioners shipping systems, products, motion, and mobile for real clients.
